# Customer Churn Analysis | Excel, SQL & Power BI

## 1. Project Overview

Customer churn is a major business challenge because losing customers can reduce revenue and increase the cost of acquiring replacements.

This project analyzes customer churn data to identify customer segments with higher churn rates, explore patterns associated with customer departures, and recommend data-driven retention strategies.

## 2. Business Objectives

- Calculate the overall customer churn rate.
- Analyze churn by contract type and customer tenure.
- Compare churn across payment methods and internet services.
- Investigate the relationship between monthly charges and churn.
- Develop practical recommendations to support customer retention.

## 3. Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, pivot tables, and charts.
- **MySQL:** SQL queries, aggregations, and business-question analysis.
- **Microsoft Power BI:** DAX measures, KPI cards, interactive dashboards, and slicers.

## 4. Key Findings

The following are approximate findings from the analysis and should be verified against the final dashboard before publication.

- Overall customer churn rate was approximately **31%**.
- Month-to-month customers had a churn rate of approximately **38%**.
- Customers using electronic check had a churn rate of approximately **36%**.
- Fiber-optic customers had a churn rate of approximately **36%**.
- Customers with monthly charges above $100 had a churn rate of approximately **36%**.
- Customers with 0–12 months of tenure had a churn rate of approximately **37.2%**.

## 5. Business Recommendations

1. Investigate onboarding and early customer experiences to identify opportunities to improve first-year retention.
2. Review cancellation reasons and customer value perceptions among month-to-month customers.
3. Examine payment setup and payment-related issues among electronic-check customers.
4. Investigate service quality and customer satisfaction among fiber-optic customers.
5. Review plan suitability and perceived value among customers with higher monthly charges.

These recommendations are hypotheses to investigate and test; the observed patterns alone do not prove the causes of churn.

## 6. Project Workflow

1. Reviewed and cleaned the dataset.
2. Conducted exploratory data analysis in Excel.
3. Answered business questions using SQL.
4. Created DAX measures and an interactive Power BI dashboard.
5. Summarized findings and developed business recommendations.

## 7. Project Deliverables

- Excel analysis workbook
- SQL query file
- Power BI dashboard (`.pbix`)
- Customer churn analysis report (PDF)

## 8. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Excel, SQL, and Power BI can be used together to investigate a business problem, analyze customer behavior, and communicate findings through actionable recommendations.

## 9. Limitations

The analysis identifies associations between customer characteristics and churn, but does not establish causation. Further investigation using cancellation reasons, customer feedback, and service records would help validate potential churn drivers.
